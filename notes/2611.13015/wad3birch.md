When AI helps people plan things like rostering and scheduling, key information often exists only in the person's head. This paper uses an optimization solver to compute exactly how much each piece of missing information is "worth", and tests whether the AI can ask for the few pieces that actually affect the decision.

Each item has a request (visible), a record store (visible), hidden information, value labels, and ground-truth questions plus a scorer.

Scoring has two parts: scoring the plan (checking each constraint), and scoring the questions.

The data comes from existing optimization problems.
