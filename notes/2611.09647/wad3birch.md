Starts from the mental-health domain: evaluates whether LLMs can recognize risk in multi-turn mental-health conversations. Traditional evaluations give the AI the complete chat log at once. The problems are: if the AI initially judges the situation as not serious, it keeps asking questions in the "not serious" direction and never gets the key information; and the further the conversation goes, the more the AI trusts its initial judgment, so even when key information appears it rarely changes its mind.
