Scale AI's second benchmark after LHAW.

Obstacles are planted by hand in coding tasks (SWE-Bench Pro) and database tasks (BIRD), along three aspects: missing information, ambiguity, and self-contradiction. What sets it apart is that the obstacles can only be discovered by actually working on the task.

For every item, domain experts modify both the task description and the task environment, and each modified item goes through 5–6 rounds of human review.
